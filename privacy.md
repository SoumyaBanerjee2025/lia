# Privacy Policy for Lia

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Lia is a personal assistant application. It is used by one person, its developer, to access his own Google account. It has no other users.

## What Lia accesses

With the developer's own consent, Lia accesses his own Google account data: Gmail messages and settings, Calendar events, Drive files, Docs, Sheets, Contacts and Tasks.

## How that data is used

Data is used only to carry out the developer's own instructions, such as reading, searching, organising, sending or deleting his own mail, files and calendar entries.

## Storage and sharing

Lia runs on the developer's own computer. Google user data is processed in memory to answer his requests. It is not sold, not shared with any third party, and not used for advertising, profiling or training any model. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted on that computer and nowhere else.

## Retention and deletion

Lia keeps no copies of Google user data. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately and permanently ends Lia's access.

## Contact

Questions can be sent to the developer at the support email address shown on the application's Google consent screen.
